Social Events

Social Events

  • The conference social dinner will take place on Friday, September 18th, at Vlachos Tavern , a traditional family-run Cypriot tavern located in Pyla, just outside Larnaca. The evening will offer participants the opportunity to enjoy traditional Cypriot cuisine and local flavours in a relaxed and welcoming setting, while continuing discussions and socialising with fellow conference participants beyond the scientific programme. Transportation to and from the dinner venue will be arranged for conference participants.
